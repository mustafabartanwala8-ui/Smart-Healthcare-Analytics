About the project

This project looks at how patient characteristics relate to recorded heart disease, diabetes, and stroke. It was built in Tableau as a dashboard and a nine-step story, and this site turns those charts into a web page.

Results describe patterns in this dataset. They do not prove cause and effect, and they are not medical advice.

Dataset
Smart healthcare dataset: 5,000 patients, 16 fields
Measures
Age, BMI, exercise level, smoking, alcohol, blood pressure, cholesterol, glucose, fatigue, chest pain, dizziness, heart disease, diabetes, stroke
Groups used
Age: 18–29, 30–44, 45–59, 60+. BMI: underweight, normal range, overweight, obesity range
Tools
Tableau, HTML, CSS, JavaScript

Key insights

  • Age stands out

    No heart disease is recorded for anyone under 45. It appears in 5.8% of patients aged 45–59 and 87.6% of those aged 60+.

  • Smoking and alcohol

    The gaps are small: 31.2% of smokers and 28.7% of non-smokers have heart disease, and 30.3% of alcohol consumers versus 29.6% of non-consumers.

  • Glucose and cholesterol

    Average glucose is 136.0 with diabetes and 133.7 without. Average cholesterol is 225.9 with heart disease and 224.1 without.

  • Age and smoking together

    Among patients aged 60+, heart disease is recorded for 91.7% of smokers and 83.6% of non-smokers. Below 45, it is 0% in both groups.

  • BMI and symptoms

    Most patients fall in the normal range (2,010) or overweight range (1,671). Chest pain is split almost evenly: 2,520 patients with it, 2,480 without.

Two cautions. The health risk score field is 100 for every patient, so it is left out of every chart. The values are also unusually uniform and heart disease is almost entirely a 60+ pattern, which suggests the data may be synthetic. Treat the findings as a learning exercise.
